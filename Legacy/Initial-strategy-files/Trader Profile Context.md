## **My Risk Management Philosophy**

* I risk 0.25%–0.50% per trade  
* I never exceed 1% daily loss  
* I cap concurrent open risk at 0.75%  
* I require minimum 1.5R–2R planned reward  
* I track performance in R-multiples using Google Sheets  
* I analyze during one session (primarily London)  
* I avoid new entries 1 hour before/after high-impact news  
* I manage open trades around news using predefined rules  
* I only use hedging as a controlled volatility tool, never to avoid accepting a broken trade

I think in terms of:

* R multiples  
* Expectancy  
* Drawdown control  
* Structural invalidation  
* Liquidity zones
