## Purpose

This plan replaces the old `5-day deadline` version.

It is built to match the first phase of the `12-week` edge curriculum.

Its role is to use `The Art and Science of Technical Analysis` as the structural backbone for:

- market-condition reading
- level quality judgment
- pullback quality judgment
- breakout vs failed-breakout judgment
- opportunity filtering

This is not a speed-reading plan.

It is an extraction plan.

## Where This Fits In The 12-Week Curriculum

This book carries the heaviest load in:

- `Weeks 1-2: Market Structure Foundation`
- `Weeks 3-4: Pullbacks, Breakouts, and Failed Moves`

1. your definitions of `trend`, `range`, and `transition`

## Core Reading Order

### Phase 1: Build Market Structure Judgment

Primary objective:
- learn to classify what the market is doing before thinking about entries

Read first:
- `Chapter 2: The Market Cycle and the Four Trades`
  - `Wyckoff’s Market Cycle`
  - `The Four Trades`
- `Chapter 3: On Trends`
  - `The Fundamental Pattern`
  - `Trend Structure`
  - `A Deeper Look at Pullbacks`
  - `Trend Analysis`
- `Chapter 4: On Trading Ranges`
  - `Support and Resistance`
  - `Trading Ranges as Functional Structures`

Your focus:
- trend vs range vs transition
- swing integrity
- meaningful support / resistance
- healthy pullback vs weak pullback

Primary chart tasks:
- label `EURUSD`, `GBPUSD`, `USDJPY` on `Daily` and `4H`
- classify each chart:
  - `trend`
  - `range`
  - `transition`
- mark:
  - last major swing high / low
  - nearest meaningful support / resistance
  - whether the chart has room

Outputs expected by the end of this phase:
- your own definitions of `trend`, `range`, and `transition`
- one page of continuation disqualifiers
- one page of meaningful-level traits

### Phase 2: Learn Failure, Transition, and Opportunity Quality

Primary objective:
- stop confusing visible movement with valid opportunity

Read next:
- `Chapter 5: Interfaces between Trends and Ranges`
  - `Breakout Trade: Trading Range to Trend`
  - `Trend to Trading Range`
  - `Trend to Opposite Trend (Trend Reversal)`
  - `Trend to Same Trend (Failure of Trend Reversal)`
- `Chapter 6: Practical Trading Templates`
  - `Failure Test`
  - `Pullback, Buying Support or Shorting Resistance`
  - `Pullback, Entering Lower Time Frame Breakout`
  - `Trading Complex Pullbacks`
  - `Failed Breakouts`

Your focus:
- breakout vs failed breakout
- reversal vs failed reversal
- continuation vs exhaustion
- complex pullbacks
- tradeable vs visible setups

Primary chart tasks:
- review `4H` and `30m`
- find examples of:
  - clean continuation pullback
  - failed pullback
  - real breakout
  - failed breakout
  - sweep / rejection

Outputs expected by the end of this phase:
- healthy pullback checklist
- failed pullback checklist
- breakout / failed-breakout decision tree
- first valid-opportunity scorecard

### Phase 3: Turn Structure Into Trade Design

Primary objective:
- connect chart reading to actual trade planning

Read after that:
- `Chapter 8: Trade Management`
  - `Placing the Initial Stop`
  - `Setting Price Targets`
  - `Active Management`
- `Chapter 9: Risk Management`
  - `Risk and Position Sizing`
  - `Misunderstood Risk`
  - `Practical Risks in Trading`
- `Chapter 10: Trade Examples`
  - `Trend Continuation`
  - `Trend Termination`
  - `Failure Test Failures`
  - `Trading at Support and Resistance`

Optional reinforcement:
- `Chapter 1: The Trader’s Edge`
  - `Defining a Trading Edge`
  - `Price Action and Market Structure on Charts`

Your focus:
- stop placement
- target realism
- `R` thinking
- trade selection quality
- why a setup can look good but still fail your framework

Primary chart tasks:
- take `10-20` historical setups
- define:
  - entry
  - stop
  - target
  - estimated `R`
  - grade
- reject any setup that does not make structural and expectancy sense

Outputs expected by the end of this phase:
- first signal-quality scorecard
- minimum acceptable setup standard
- top reasons to skip a trade

## How To Take Notes

For each reading day:

1. extract the main concept
2. write what weak opportunity it helps you reject
3. review `3` charts only
4. pick one best example
5. write one rule

Do not write long notes just to feel productive.

The note is successful only if it sharpens chart decisions.

## Questions This Book Must Help You Answer

By the end of this plan, you should answer these more clearly:

- Is the market trending, ranging, or transitioning?
- Is this level meaningful or random?
- Is this pullback healthy or failed?
- Is this breakout real or likely to fail?
- Is this opportunity valid, borderline, or skip?
- Where is the real invalidation?
- Is there enough room for the trade to make sense?

- better filters

- better opportunity selection
