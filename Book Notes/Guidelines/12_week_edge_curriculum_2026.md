# 12-Week Edge Curriculum 2026

## Objective

This curriculum is for the period before active FTMO preparation.

Its purpose is to build:

- better market judgment
- better opportunity selection
- better structural thinking
- better risk thinking
- better execution discipline

It is reading-heavy by design, but every week must still include chart work.

## Core Principle

Reading should improve your decisions on live charts.

If a week produces no clearer answers to these questions, the week was not successful:

- what condition is the market in
- is price at a meaningful level
- is this a valid opportunity or just visual temptation
- where does the idea clearly fail
- is the reward worth the risk

## Time Budget

Default weekly mix:

- `4` reading sessions
- `2` chart annotation / replay sessions
- `1` consolidation session

Recommended ratio:

- `60%` reading and notes
- `25%` chart work
- `15%` replay / review

## Book Priority

Core sequence:

1. `The Art and Science of Technical Analysis`
2. `Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets` selected chapters
3. `Forex Patterns and Probabilities`
4. `The New Trading for a Living`
5. `Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom`
6. `Trading Price Action Trends` selected sections
7. `Naked Forex` targeted review only
8. `Trading in the Zone`
9. `The Disciplined Trader`

## Required Weekly Output

Every week must produce:

1. one page of concept notes
2. one page of chart examples
3. one checklist or scorecard improvement
4. one rule you will apply going forward

## Weeks 1-2: Market Structure Foundation

Primary source:

- `The Art and Science of Technical Analysis`

Secondary source:

- `Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets` selected trend / support-resistance sections

Focus:

- market cycle
- trend structure
- trend vs range vs transition
- support and resistance
- swing integrity

Chart work:

- review `Daily` and `4H` on `EURUSD`, `GBPUSD`, `USDJPY`
- label each chart:
  - `trend`
  - `range`
  - `transition`
- mark:
  - last major swing high / low
  - nearest meaningful support / resistance
  - whether the chart has room

Deliverables by end of Week 2:

- your own definitions of `trend`, `range`, and `transition`
- one page of signs that disqualify continuation trades
- one page of signs that make a level meaningful

## Weeks 3-4: Pullbacks, Breakouts, and Failed Moves

Primary source:

- `The Art and Science of Technical Analysis`

Secondary source:

- `Forex Patterns and Probabilities`

Focus:

- healthy pullbacks
- failed pullbacks
- breakout vs failed breakout
- trend continuation vs exhaustion
- reversal attempts vs failed reversals

Chart work:

- review `4H` and `30m`
- find examples of:
  - clean continuation pullback
  - failed pullback
  - real breakout
  - failed breakout
  - liquidity sweep and rejection

Deliverables by end of Week 4:

- healthy pullback checklist
- failed pullback checklist
- breakout / failed breakout decision tree
- first version of a valid-opportunity scorecard

## Weeks 5-6: FX Context and Opportunity Selection

Primary source:

- `Forex Patterns and Probabilities`

Secondary source:

- `Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets` selected chapters

Focus:

- FX-specific trend and range behavior
- continuation context
- reversal context
- pair selection
- how to think probabilistically about scenarios

Chart work:

- pre-session marking on your target pairs
- write two scenarios per pair:
  - continuation
  - sweep / reversal

Deliverables by end of Week 6:

- preferred pair shortlist
- session-based watchlist template
- list of invalid opportunities you keep getting attracted to

## Weeks 7-8: Process, Trade Design, and Risk

Primary source:

- `The New Trading for a Living`

Secondary source:

- `Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom`

Focus:

- trade planning
- journaling
- rating trades
- expectancy
- position sizing
- system design

Chart work:

- take `10-20` historical setups
- define:
  - entry
  - stop
  - target
  - grade
  - estimated `R`
- reject any setup that does not make structural and expectancy sense

Deliverables by end of Week 8:

- first formal trade plan template
- first formal journal template
- minimum setup standard
- clear risk policy

## Weeks 9-10: Tactical Reinforcement Without Trigger Addiction

Primary source:

- `Trading Price Action Trends` selected sections

Secondary sources:

- `High Probability Trading Strategies`
- `Naked Forex` targeted sections only

Focus:

- trend leg quality
- continuation behavior
- rejection behavior
- trigger clarity
- difference between visible pattern and tradeable opportunity

Important rule:

- do not let these books redefine the framework
- they are here to sharpen execution, not replace structure

Chart work:

- compare similar-looking setups and separate:
  - valid
  - borderline
  - skip

Deliverables by end of Week 10:

- trigger quality checklist
- list of your preferred trigger variants
- list of pattern traps you must avoid

## Weeks 11-12: Psychology and Execution Discipline

Primary source:

- `Trading in the Zone`

Secondary source:

- `The Disciplined Trader`

Focus:

- uncertainty
- hesitation
- need for certainty
- rule-following
- emotional distortion after wins and losses

Chart work:

- review your own passed, forced, and hypothetical trades
- identify whether the issue was:
  - weak read
  - weak discipline
  - weak risk thinking

Deliverables by end of Week 12:

- anti-hesitation checklist
- anti-FOMO checklist
- post-loss protocol
- one-page execution policy

## Daily Study Template

Use this template each study day:

1. what did I read
2. what concept matters most
3. what does that concept help me reject
4. one chart example where it applies
5. one chart example where I would have misread it before
6. one rule extracted

## Weekly Review Questions

At the end of each week, answer:

1. What did I understand better this week?
2. What weak opportunities can I now reject faster?
3. What still feels unclear?
4. What concept needs chart repetition, not more reading?
5. Did I improve my framework, or just consume information?

## What To Skip For Now

Do not spend core study time on:

- books with many strategies but weak filtering
- books with heavy marketing tone
- books that mostly repeat trigger patterns without improving judgment
- books that make you feel productive without making you more selective

## End State

By the end of `12` weeks, you should have:

- a clear market-condition framework
- a pullback and breakout framework
- a valid-opportunity scorecard
- a session-based watchlist routine
- a formal trade plan and journal template
- a clearer understanding of which setups belong in your final strategy

## Rule For Book Selection Going Forward

A new book is worth your time only if it improves one of these:

- context reading
- opportunity filtering
- risk design
- execution discipline

If it mostly offers more setups, it is low priority.
