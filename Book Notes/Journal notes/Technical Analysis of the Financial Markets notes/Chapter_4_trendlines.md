Section focus:
- `Trendlines`

## Core Idea

- Trendlines are simple but useful tools for visualizing direction, slope, and structural integrity.
- Their value comes from framing trend behavior, not from forcing exact touches.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should use trendlines as context tools, not as magical signals.
- A broken trendline is a warning, but not always full proof of reversal by itself.

## Rule Extracted

- `Use trendlines to frame trend speed and structural integrity, but confirm breaks with actual price behavior.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- Trendlines can help with visual pullback structure, but they should remain secondary to actual swing points and invalidation levels in your setup work.
