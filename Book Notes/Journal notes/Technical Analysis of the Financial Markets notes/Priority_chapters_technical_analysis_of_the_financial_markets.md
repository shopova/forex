## Main Takeaway

- In your reading framework, this book is a `reference layer`, not a primary backbone.
- Its value is broad technical literacy:
  - classic trend language
  - support/resistance and pattern vocabulary
  - moving-average structure
  - money-management basics
  - enough intermarket context to avoid narrow chart blindness
- It should support `The Art and Science of Technical Analysis`, not replace it.

## How To Use These Notes

- Read these chapters selectively, then compare them against your Grimes notes.
- Keep what improves:
  - market-condition reading
  - level quality judgment
  - continuation vs reversal judgment
  - risk and trade-structure thinking
- Downgrade anything that:

  - weakens your rejection criteria

## What This Book Adds To Your Framework

- cleaner classic chart vocabulary
- broader visual recognition of reversal and continuation structures
- a more conventional language for trend and average-based analysis
- reinforcement on money management

## Practical Forex Translation

- This book is most useful for:
  - seeing trend strength and maturity
  - organizing support/resistance and pattern language
  - using moving averages as structure aids
  - avoiding narrow single-pattern thinking
- It is less useful if treated as:
  - a trigger book
  - an indicator book
  - a replacement for your structure-first framework
