Section focus:
- `Speed Resistance Lines`

## Core Idea

- Speed resistance lines are another way to judge how much of a prior move is being retraced.
- They focus on the rate of ascent or descent, not just the existence of a pullback.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should see them as an analytical aid, not as a required tool.
- Their main value is helping me think about trend speed and correction depth together.

## Rule Extracted

- `Any tool that helps measure how aggressively a trend is being retraced can improve context, as long as it does not override clearer structure.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- You probably do not need to use speedlines explicitly, but the concept is useful: fast retracement against a move is usually more dangerous than slow, corrective drift.
