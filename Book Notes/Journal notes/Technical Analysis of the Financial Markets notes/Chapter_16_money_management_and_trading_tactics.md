Chapter focus:
- `Chapter 16: Money Management and Trading Tactics`

## Core Idea

- Technical analysis is not enough on its own.
- Trade survival depends on:
  - position sizing
  - stop discipline
  - tactical realism
  - consistency of execution

## Main Concepts

- `Protect capital first`
  - No analysis edge matters if trade size or stop behavior is reckless.

- `Stops and risk limits`
  - The market will be wrong often enough that losses must be predefined and accepted.

- `Position sizing`
  - Size should reflect account risk, not conviction alone.

- `Tactical discipline`
  - Entering, scaling, and managing should be planned rather than improvised.

## What This Changes For Me

- Good chart reading is still not enough without consistent risk handling.
- A strong-looking setup can still be a bad trade if the stop, size, or management is poor.
- My real edge includes how I structure the trade, not only how I identify it.

## Rule Extracted

- `The technical idea creates opportunity, but money management decides whether that opportunity survives contact with real trading.`
