# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Internal Trendlines`

## Core Idea

- Not every useful structural line has to connect the absolute extreme highs or lows.
- Sometimes internal price structure offers a cleaner view of the trend than the outermost spike.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should not become rigid about anchoring only to absolute wicks.
- Sometimes the market body of movement matters more than one exceptional probe.

## Rule Extracted

- `Use the line that best explains the actual structure of price, not just the most extreme point on the chart.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is useful when FX charts print one-off sweeps or news spikes that distort the cleaner underlying trend geometry.
