Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Trend Has Three Classifications`

## Core Idea

- Trends exist on multiple scales.
- Major, secondary, and minor trends help explain why lower-time-frame action can temporarily move against the broader move.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should always ask which time-frame trend I am trading and which higher-time-frame trend may still dominate.
- Time-frame conflict is not random noise; it is structural context.

## Rule Extracted

- `A lower-time-frame move must always be judged in the context of the higher-time-frame trend classification.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This supports your multi-time-frame reading directly: a 1H pullback can exist inside a 4H trend without invalidating the bigger move.
