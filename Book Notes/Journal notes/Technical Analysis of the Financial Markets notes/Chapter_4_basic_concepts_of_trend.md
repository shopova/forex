## Main Takeaway

- Trend is the first structural question.
- Before thinking about setup type, the trader must decide:
  - is price trending
  - in what direction
  - with what degree of strength
  - and whether that trend is intact or weakening

## Section Coverage

- `Definition of Trend`
  - Trend is defined by the sequence of peaks and troughs.
  - Structure comes before opinion.

- `Trend Has Three Directions`
  - Markets can move up, down, or sideways.
  - Sideways is a real condition, not just an inconvenience.

- `Trend Has Three Classifications`
  - Major, secondary, and minor trends help organize time-frame relevance.

- `Support and Resistance`
  - Old turning points matter because they shape future decision zones.

- `Trendlines`
  - Trendlines visualize slope, continuity, and likely invalidation.

- `The Fan Principle`
  - Repeatedly broken trendlines can show progressive weakening.

- `The Importance of the Number Three`
  - Repetition and third tests often matter in technical work.

- `The Relative Steepness of the Trendline`
  - Very steep trends are often less durable.

- `The Channel Line`
  - Parallel structure helps frame trend boundaries and exhaustion warnings.

- `Percentage Retracements`
  - Retracement depth helps separate correction from possible reversal.

- `Speed Resistance Lines`
  - Another way to judge correction depth and trend speed.

- `Gann and Fibonacci Fan Lines`
  - Alternative fan frameworks for trend and retracement geometry.

- `Internal Trendlines`
  - Not every useful line sits on the absolute extreme.

- `Reversal Days`
  - Sharp intraday rejection at extremes can signal a shift in control.

- `Price Gaps`
  - Gaps can reveal urgency, exhaustion, or discontinuity in trend behavior.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should classify trend first and trigger second.
- A trade idea is weaker if I cannot explain the current trend condition clearly.
- Structure matters more than a single candle or signal bar.

## Rule Extracted

- `If I cannot define the trend clearly from swing structure, I should not pretend I have a clean directional edge.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This supports your higher-time-frame-first process directly.
- On FX pairs, the main use is:
  - identify whether the pair is trending or just oscillating
  - locate the last meaningful swing high and low
  - decide whether continuation logic is still valid
- This chapter should reinforce your `trend / range / transition` work, not replace it with generic trendline trading.
