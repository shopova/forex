# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `The Channel Line`

## Core Idea

- Channels help frame how price behaves within a trend.
- The opposite side of the channel can act as a boundary for extension, warning, or profit-taking.

## What This Changes For Me

- I can use channels to judge whether price is extending normally, failing to reach resistance/support, or pressing too aggressively.
- Channel failure can warn that trend quality is changing.

## Rule Extracted

- `Use channel behavior to judge trend health: clean travel within the channel supports continuation, while failure or violent expansion can warn of change.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- Channels can help you judge whether a move still has orderly structure or is becoming climax-like and lower quality for fresh entries.
