# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `The Relative Steepness of the Trendline`

## Core Idea

- Steeper trends are often less sustainable.
- The sharper the angle, the more likely the move is to be unstable and vulnerable to correction.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should become more cautious when a move becomes too steep too quickly.
- Strong slope can mean strength, but it can also mean fragility.

## Rule Extracted

- `When a trend becomes unusually steep, treat continuation with more caution because the move may be less durable.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is relevant to late-session spikes, news-driven runs, and parabolic FX moves where the angle itself becomes a warning sign.
