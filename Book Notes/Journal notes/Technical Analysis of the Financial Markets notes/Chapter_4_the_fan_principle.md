# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `The Fan Principle`

## Core Idea

- When successive trendlines keep breaking, the trend may be progressively weakening.
- The fan principle treats multiple broken lines as evidence of changing momentum and possible reversal.

## What This Changes For Me

- One broken line is not always enough.
- Repeated loss of slope is more informative than a single dramatic line break.

## Rule Extracted

- `A trend that keeps losing steeper support or resistance lines is showing progressive weakness, not clean continuation.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is useful when an FX move keeps accelerating and then starts failing to hold its steepest structure. That is often a warning to stop chasing continuation late.
