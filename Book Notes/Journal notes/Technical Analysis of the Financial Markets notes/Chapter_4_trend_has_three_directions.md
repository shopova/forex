# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Trend Has Three Directions`

## Core Idea

- Markets do not only go up or down.
- Sideways movement is a third condition and must be treated as its own environment.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should not force a bullish or bearish bias when the market is actually ranging.
- Sideways is often where weak trades are born.

## Rule Extracted

- `Up, down, and sideways are all real market states; forcing direction in a sideways market creates low-quality trades.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This reinforces your rule to avoid middle-of-range trades and to classify `trend / range / transition` before looking for triggers.
