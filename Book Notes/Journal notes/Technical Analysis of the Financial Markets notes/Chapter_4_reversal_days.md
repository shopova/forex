# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Reversal Days`

## Core Idea

- Reversal days are sharp rejection sessions that can warn of a short-term shift in control.
- They matter most when they appear in the right location.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should not treat every large reversal candle as equally important.
- Location and prior trend condition determine whether the reversal day actually matters.

## Rule Extracted

- `A reversal day is meaningful only when it appears at a structurally important place and changes the market's behavior, not just its candle shape.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This fits your interest in rejection behavior and big-shadow-style candles, but it reinforces that context comes before the bar itself.
