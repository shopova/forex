# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Definition of Trend`

## Core Idea

- Trend is defined by structure, not by opinion.
- The basic question is whether highs and lows are progressing in a directional sequence or not.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should stop calling any directional burst a trend without checking swing structure.
- If structure is unclear, my setup quality is automatically weaker.

## Rule Extracted

- `Define the trend from peaks and troughs first; everything else comes after that.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- On FX charts, this means identifying whether the pair is truly printing higher highs/higher lows or lower highs/lower lows before applying continuation logic.
