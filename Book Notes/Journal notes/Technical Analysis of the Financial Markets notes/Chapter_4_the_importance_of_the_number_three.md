Section focus:
- `The Importance of the Number Three`

## Core Idea

- Technical analysis often gives extra weight to third tests, third pushes, or three-part structures.
- Repetition can matter because it shows a market testing and re-testing the same condition.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should pay attention when a market reaches a third push or third test into a level.
- Repetition can strengthen either continuation or exhaustion interpretation depending on context.

## Rule Extracted

- `Third tests and third pushes often carry more information than the first or second attempt, especially near extremes.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This supports your reading of repeated sweeps, multiple tests of highs/lows, and late-stage trend behavior around key zones.
