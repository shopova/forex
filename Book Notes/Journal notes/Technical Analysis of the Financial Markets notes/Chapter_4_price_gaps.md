# Section Note: Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets - A Comprehensive Guide to Trading Methods and Applications 1999.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: Basic Concepts of Trend`

Section focus:
- `Price Gaps`

## Core Idea

- Gaps can reveal urgency, imbalance, exhaustion, or transition.
- They deserve special attention because they show discontinuity in price acceptance.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should treat gaps as information about urgency, not just empty chart space.
- Some gaps strengthen trend logic; others warn of exhaustion or instability.

## Rule Extracted

- `A gap is a message about urgency and imbalance; its meaning depends on where it appears in the larger structure.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- FX spot charts show fewer true gaps than stocks, but the concept still matters around weekend opens, session transitions, and sudden repricing after news.
