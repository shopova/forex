## Core Idea

- Continuation patterns are pauses inside an existing directional move.
- They are valuable because they help distinguish:
  - normal consolidation
  - healthy pullback
  - and early evidence that the trend may actually be failing

## Main Concepts

- `Flags, pennants, rectangles, triangles`
  - All are different ways price can pause before the next leg.
  - The common logic is more important than the name:
    - trend first
    - pause second
    - follow-through third

- `Context before pattern`
  - A continuation pattern is stronger after a real impulse.
  - Without prior directional strength, the same shape may just be random congestion.

- `Breakout quality`
  - A valid continuation pattern should resolve with urgency.
  - Weak breakout behavior is a warning sign.

- `Projection logic`
  - Classic measured-move thinking can help estimate whether enough room exists for the continuation idea.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should stop reading every small pause as continuation.
- A continuation pattern is meaningful only if it comes after imbalance and resolves cleanly.
- If the breakout from the pause is hesitant, I should become more skeptical quickly.

## Rule Extracted

- `A continuation pattern is only valid when it forms inside a real trend and breaks with behavior that proves the pause was absorption, not indecision.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This chapter fits your continuation work well:
  - break + retest
  - pullback continuation
  - post-impulse pause
- The main FX use is to judge whether the market is:
  - pausing cleanly for continuation
  - or churning into range conditions
- It supports your existing rule that middle-of-box noise and overlapping structure should usually be skipped.
