Section focus:
- `Failure Test`

## Core Idea

- A failure test is a brief move beyond an important level that immediately rejects and reverses.
- It is the classic `spring` / `upthrust` adapted into a concrete trading plan.
- The edge comes from trapped traders and fast price rejection away from the level.

## Setup

- A meaningful support or resistance level must exist.
- Price probes beyond it just far enough to trigger stops or breakout entries.
- The market then quickly closes back through the level with momentum.

## Trigger

- Enter when the failure is confirmed back through the level.
- Best examples should start working within `1-3` bars on the trading timeframe.
- If the trade stalls or consolidates near the level, quality drops sharply.

## Stop

- Stop goes beyond the most extreme test outside the level.
- Because this is an aggressive countertrend trade, the stop must be respected fully.
- Do not add to losers.

## Profit Target

- Sensible plan:
  - take partial profit around `1R`
  - hold a remainder for a larger correction or reversal
- Baseline expectation should be modest:
  - many become only corrections, not full trend changes

## Failure Pattern

- What should not happen:
  - price should not consolidate near the level
  - price should not linger outside the level
- If it does, breakout/continuation pressure may still be in control.

## What This Changes For Me

- This is one of the clearest risk-defined countertrend templates in the book.
- If a level fails back quickly, the reversal can be sharp.
- If the market does not reject fast, the trade quality is lower.

## Rule Extracted

- `A good failure test should reject the level quickly. If it hangs near the level, the trade is lower quality or wrong.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is the closest Chapter 6 template to many `Wammie/Moolah` and false-break range trades.
- It also overlaps with failed breakout logic around box edges.
