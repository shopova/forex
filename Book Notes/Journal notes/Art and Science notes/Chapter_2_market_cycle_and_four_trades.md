## Main Takeaway

- Chapter 2 creates the first structural lens for reading charts.
- `Wyckoff's Market Cycle` explains where the market is in broad phase terms.
- `The Four Trades` explains what type of trade is actually being attempted.
- Together they stop me from reacting to candles without knowing the market phase or trade category.

## Section Coverage

- `Wyckoff's Market Cycle`
  - Markets rotate through `accumulation`, `markup`, `distribution`, and `markdown`.
  - The transition between phases is where many false signals and weak trades appear.

- `The Four Trades`
  - Every technical trade can be framed as `trend continuation`, `trend termination`, `support/resistance holding`, or `support/resistance failing`.
  - Trade quality improves when the trade type matches the market phase.

## What This Chapter Changes

- I should classify the market before I think about the trigger.
- I should define the trade category before I think about entry.
- A strong candle means little if the phase and trade type are mismatched.

## Practical Forex Translation

- `Last Kiss` is usually a `support/resistance failing` trade.
- `Big Shadow` can be either `holding` or early `termination`, depending on context.
- `Wammie/Moolah` is usually `holding` or `termination`, not standard continuation.

## Rule Extracted

- `Label the market phase first, then define the trade type. If either is unclear, the setup is lower quality.`
