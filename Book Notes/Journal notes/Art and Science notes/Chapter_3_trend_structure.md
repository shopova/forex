Section focus:
- `Trend Structure`

## Core Idea

- Trend structure is read through swings, not opinion.
- `HH + HL` defines bullish structure and `LH + LL` defines bearish structure, but the structure has to stay internally consistent.
- `Uncertain` is a valid market state when the structure no longer reads cleanly.

## Key Points

- With-trend swings should normally dominate countertrend swings.
- A first countertrend swing that is larger than the prior with-trend swing is a major warning.
- Complex consolidations can temporarily distort simple swing logic.
- Trend lines are secondary tools; real swings come first.

## What To Look For

- `Clean signs`
  - clear swing progression
  - with-trend legs larger than corrections
  - no major conflict between current structure and higher time frame
- `Warning signs`
  - shrinking with-trend swings
  - abnormally large countertrend move
  - broken rhythm without full opposite-trend confirmation
- `Disqualifying signs`
  - trend lines are forced onto flat price action
  - chart is structurally mixed but I am pretending it is still clean

## Trading Meaning

- Best use:
  - `Trade continuation only while swing structure still supports it.`
- Avoid:
  - `Calling reversal too early just because the trend got messy once.`

## Rule Extracted

- `If the first countertrend swing becomes larger than the preceding with-trend swing, I should shift from continuation assumption to caution.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- On your `4H`, structure should decide whether a pair is trend, range, or transition before any `1H` trigger matters.
- If `HH/HL` or `LH/LL` is no longer clean, lower-risk continuation logic becomes weaker.
