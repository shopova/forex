## 2. Main Takeaway

Write `3-6` lines total:

- What is the main idea?
  - Having potential embedded in the trader's mind — be cautious in everything and do not assume the market will act the same every time.
  - A trading range is not "nothing happening." It is a functional structure where buyers and sellers are testing both sides and storing energy for a later move.
  - Support and resistance are not fixed lines with automatic meaning. Their value depends on context, location in the larger structure, and how price behaves when it reaches them.
  - The key job inside a range is not prediction. It is judging whether the market is more likely to hold, fail, trap, or stay noisy.
- What trading mistake does it help prevent?
  - Treating every touch of support or resistance as a trade.
  - Placing stops in obvious liquidity clusters where the market is likely to probe.
  - Assuming every breakout from a clean-looking box is real.
- Which weekly focus does it support?
  - `pullbacks / failed moves`
  - `psychology / execution`
  - `FX opportunity selection`

## 3. What This Helps Me Judge

Answer these briefly:

- Market condition:
  - A range is confirmed when price repeatedly fails to hold beyond support or resistance and keeps rotating back into balance.
- Level quality:
  - Better levels are tied to repeated reactions, trapped traders, and meaningful higher-time-frame structure, not just visually clean horizontal lines.
- Opportunity quality:
  - The best opportunities in ranges usually come near the edges, after `failed moves`, `springs`, `upthrusts`, or `acceptance beyond the range`, not in the middle.
- Invalidation clarity:
  - Invalidation is clearer when the trade idea is linked to a specific range behavior: `holding`, `failing`, or `failed breakout`.
- Reward-to-risk clarity:
  - Reward-to-risk improves when entry is taken near a range edge or after a false break, and worsens sharply in the middle of the box.

If one of those is not improved by the section, leave it blank.

## 4. What To Look For On Charts

Fill only short bullets.

- Clean signs:
  - Failed breakout that quickly returns back into the range.
  - Strong rejection from the edge of the box.
  - A box can be horizontal or slightly sloping; a sloping range against the higher-time-frame trend can behave like a complex pullback.
  - One side of the range keeps being probed, but price still cannot accept outside it.
- Warning signs:
  - Very clean levels attract stops and can create extra volatility and slippage.
  - Repeated tests weaken the edge if each reaction becomes less forceful.
  - Consolidation near one side of the box can precede breakout, but it can also trap early traders.
- Skip signs:
  - Price is in the middle of the range.
  - Multiple fake breakouts already occurred from the same box.
  - `Spring` or `upthrust` is visible, but the reclaim/rejection is weak.
  - `Converging ranges` like triangles, wedges, or pennants are present; treat them as separate structures, not plain horizontal ranges.

## 5. Trade Meaning

- Setup idea:
  - `Failed breakout at a range edge` or `acceptance after true breakout from a box`
- Valid only if:
  - The range is clearly defined, the reaction happens near an edge, and price shows either strong rejection back into the range or clear acceptance beyond the boundary.
- Invalid if:
  - Price is in the middle of the box, the breakout has already failed multiple times, or the reaction candle appears without room to target.
