Section focus:
- `Trading at Support and Resistance`

## Core Idea

- Support and resistance trades reduce to two families:
  - breakouts
  - failed breakouts
- The level is important, but price behavior around the level is more important than the line itself.

## Main Lessons

- `There are multiple valid breakout entries`
  - early entry inside the base
  - entry on the actual breakout
  - entry on the first post-breakout pullback
- Each has a different trade-off between location and confirmation.

- `Early entry gives strength if the breakout hesitates`
  - A trader positioned near the base has more flexibility when the first breakout attempt fails.
  - The late breakout trader is often immediately vulnerable.

- `There is nothing magical about the breakout level`
  - Pullbacks after breakout can:
    - stop at the level
    - violate the level
    - stay well above or below the level
  - The behavior matters more than whether price touched the exact line.

- `Failed breakouts can be violent`
  - Breakout traders often enter stretched markets.
  - When the break fails, trapped flow can fuel a sharp reversal.

- `Post-breakout pullbacks should usually stay simple`
  - In the best new trends, the first pullback after breakout is often not very complex.
  - If the structure becomes messy early, the breakout quality may be weaker than it first appeared.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should stop over-focusing on exact level touches.
- The real questions are:
  - did the market accept beyond the level?
  - did the pullback stay healthy?
  - did the breakout attract urgent follow-through or trap participants?

## Rule Extracted

- `At support and resistance, price behavior around the level tells the truth; the line itself does not.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This section maps directly to your breakout and retest work:
  - break + retest
  - failed break / sweep
  - continuation after base
- In FX, the best use is:
  - judge whether the level was accepted or rejected
  - treat the first pullback after breakout as a quality test
  - avoid assuming a retest is invalid just because price pierced the level slightly
