Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 4: On Trading Ranges`

Section focus:
- `Trading Ranges as Functional Structures`

## Core Idea

- A trading range is a structural function, not dead market space.
- It stores energy, tests both sides, and can lead either to continuation or to transition.
- The best trades around ranges usually come from the edges or from real acceptance outside them.

## Key Points

- Ranges can be horizontal or slightly sloped.
- One side of a range may be tested repeatedly before it finally fails.
- A tight area near one edge of the larger range often matters more than the middle.
- Most weak trades in ranges happen because the trader enters where there is no structural edge.

## What To Look For

- `Clean signs`
  - edge reactions
  - failed moves back into the range
  - real acceptance outside the box
- `Warning signs`
  - repeated fake breakouts
  - price living near the middle
  - too much chop with no directional pressure
- `Disqualifying signs`
  - trading inside the center of the range
  - confusing triangles or wedges with plain ranges

## Trading Meaning

- Best use:
  - `Trade failed breakout, edge rejection, or true breakout acceptance.`
- Avoid:
  - `Middle-of-box trades and repeated attempts from the same weakening edge.`

## Rule Extracted

- `The edge in a range is near the edge of the range, not in the middle.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This section supports box-based logic like `Last Kiss`, but only when the box is functional and the breakout / retest behavior is real.
- If the range already produced multiple fake breaks, quality drops sharply.
