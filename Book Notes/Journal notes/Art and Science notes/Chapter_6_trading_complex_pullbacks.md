Section focus:
- `Trading Complex Pullbacks`

## Core Idea

- Complex pullbacks are two-legged pullbacks inside a larger trend.
- They are common, especially in mature trends.
- Many traders lose on simple pullbacks only to discover the market was building a better complex continuation.

## Setup

- Structure is basically an `ABCD` pullback inside the trend.
- Sometimes the complex structure is obvious on the trading timeframe.
- Sometimes it is hidden and only hinted at by one or two with-trend candles inside an otherwise simple-looking pullback.

## Trigger

- Standard pullback triggers still apply:
  - enter near the end of the second leg
  - or use breakout/confirmation logic from inside the pullback
- A measured move can often help estimate where the second pullback leg may terminate.

## Stop

- Complex pullbacks often give clearer stops than simple pullbacks because the second leg defines risk better.
- Stops just beyond the second-leg termination are often reasonable.

## Profit Target

- Same pullback targets apply.
- Grimes suggests complex pullbacks can justify pressing slightly more for stronger continuation compared with simple pullbacks.

## Main Challenge

- Risk management after being stopped on the first simple pullback attempt.
- The trader needs a plan:
  - whether to reenter
  - how much risk remains acceptable
  - how to avoid revenge trading

## Rule Extracted

- `A stopped-out simple pullback can become a valid complex pullback; loss on the first attempt does not automatically invalidate the larger continuation thesis.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This matters directly for your continuation strategy.
- A failed first continuation entry is not always a reason to abandon the trend; sometimes it is the beginning of the cleaner second-leg pullback.
