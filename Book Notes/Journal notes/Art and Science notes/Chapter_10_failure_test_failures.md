# Section Note: Art and Science of Technical Analysis

Source:
- `Reading-materials/some-investment-books/New Folder/The Art and Science of Technical Analysis - Market Structure, Price Action, and Trading Strategies 2012 - annotated.pdf`

Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 10: Trade Examples`

Section focus:
- `Failure Test Failures`

## Core Idea

- Failure tests can fail in ways that are slow, sloppy, or violent.
- The biggest mistake is staying emotionally attached after the pattern stops behaving correctly.
- When these trades do not move quickly, the trader must reduce risk, scratch the trade, or accept the stop cleanly.

## Main Lessons

- `Missed first target is a warning`
  - Failure tests usually have tight stops and close first targets.
  - If price cannot reach that target quickly, something is wrong.

- `Consolidation can be failure`
  - A drift or flat consolidation after entry can be enough evidence that the pattern is not working.
  - Hope is not a management plan.

- `Second entries are sometimes mandatory`
  - A clean first stop-out can be followed by a very good second entry.
  - This is only possible if the first loss was controlled properly.

- `Gap risk is real`
  - Because these trades sit near trend extremes, continuation gaps against the position can produce outsized losses.
  - Reduced size is often the rational response.

- `Profit-taking discipline matters`
  - If the first target is hit, profits must actually be booked.
  - Letting a trade touch the first target and then turn into a full-size loser is poor execution, not bad luck.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should judge failure tests by speed and quality of follow-through, not by the elegance of the entry.
- A trade that lingers after entry is often no longer the same trade I thought I entered.
- If I cannot take the second entry because the first loss was mishandled, the real mistake happened earlier.

## Rule Extracted

- `If a failure test does not move quickly or cleanly, reduce risk fast; control of the first loss preserves the real opportunity.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This matters for failed-break and sweep trades in FX:
  - if price reclaims a level but then just churns, that is a warning
  - if the move cannot separate from the level, the trap may not be real
  - a clean stop-out can still leave room for a second valid entry later
- It also reinforces your need to manage reversal-style trades more conservatively than continuation trades.
