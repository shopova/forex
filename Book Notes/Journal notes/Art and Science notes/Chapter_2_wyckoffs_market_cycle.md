## 1. Core Idea

- What is the main concept from today?
  - The market tends to rotate through four repeating phases: `accumulation`, `markup`, `distribution`, and `markdown`.
  - The edge is not in predicting every turn early, but in identifying which phase price is most likely in now.
- What market problem is this concept solving?
  - It gives a practical framework for separating `trend`, `range`, and `transition`, so entries are taken in context instead of reacting to isolated candles.

## 2. Market Condition Lens

Based on today's reading, how should the market be classified?

- Trend:
  - `Markup` and `markdown` are directional phases with persistent expansion, cleaner swings, and follow-through after pullbacks.
- Range:
  - `Accumulation` and `distribution` often appear as sideways structures where price is being absorbed before the next directional move.
- Transition:
  - The shift from one phase to the next is the most deceptive part of the cycle; this is where breakouts fail, reversals start, or trends lose quality.
  - Wyckoff `spring` and `upthrust` - a bar that tests below/above a level and immediately finds buyers/sellers.

Specific visual clues:

- Clean signs:
  - Repeated defense of a horizontal zone.
  - Clear swing progression in one direction once trend begins.
  - Price acceptance above prior resistance in `markup`, or below prior support in `markdown`.
  - Pullbacks that stay controlled instead of fully retracing the prior move.
  - Candles touching support with shadow only can be a sign on `accumulation`.
- Warning signs:
  - Overlapping candles after a strong directional leg.
  - Breaks that do not hold outside the range.
  - Sudden expansion late in trend without clean continuation.
- Disqualifying signs:
  - Mixed structure with no clear directional control.
  - Price trapped in the middle of a box with no edge.
  - Entry trigger appearing without room to the next major level.

## 3. Setup Interpretation

- Setup name:
  - `Market phase classification before trigger selection`
- What must be true before the setup is valid:
  - A clear phase can be labeled from the four-part cycle.
  - The chart shows whether price is `building`, `trending`, or `distributing`.
  - The trade idea aligns with the phase instead of fighting it.
- What makes the setup weak:
  - Treating every range as accumulation.
  - Treating every sharp rally as fresh markup when it may be late distribution.
  - Ignoring whether the move is clean or increasingly overlapping.
- What would invalidate the idea:
  - The supposed breakout is rejected back into the range.
  - The swing structure fails to continue in the expected direction.
  - Risk-to-reward is poor because the next opposing zone is too close.

## 7. What I Misread Or Almost Misread

- A sideways pause after a rally is not automatically `distribution`; it can be healthy consolidation inside `markup`.
- A breakout bar is not enough by itself; the real read comes from whether price is accepted outside the prior range.
- The hardest mistake is confusing `transition` with a clean new trend too early.

## 8. Rule Extracted Today

## Practical Forex Translation

- Use `Daily` and `4H` first to decide whether the pair is in `trend`, `range`, or `transition`.
- Take `Last Kiss`, `Big Shadow`, or `Wammie/Moolah` only when the trigger matches the phase.
- Be cautious with breakout trades during `transition`; this is where fake breaks are most common.
- Favor setups that have clear room to the next level and can realistically deliver at least `2R`.
