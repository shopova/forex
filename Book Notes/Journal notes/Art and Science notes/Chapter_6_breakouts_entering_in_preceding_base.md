Section focus:
- `Breakouts, Entering in the Preceding Base`

## Core Idea

- This is an anticipatory breakout entry.
- Instead of paying the actual breakout, the trader enters inside the prebreakout base to get better location and avoid slippage.

## Setup

- A meaningful breakout level must exist.
- Good breakout levels are visible and important to many traders.
- The market often forms a `breakout base` before the move:
  - tight range near the edge of a larger range
  - higher lows pressing into resistance
  - clear accumulation/distribution behavior

## Trigger

- Three practical entry styles:
  - bid near the bottom of the base
  - enter on springs / upthrusts within the base
  - establish position anywhere inside the base once the formation is valid
- Trade-off:
  - better location usually means more missed winners if using only limit orders at the bottom

## Stop

- Two-part trade management problem:
  - before breakout happens
  - after breakout happens
- Before breakout:
  - expect noise and occasional brief failures under the base
- After breakout:
  - do not assume the actual breakout level must hold perfectly

## Profit Target

- Grimes generally does not favor fixed profit targets on the best breakout trades.
- Better idea:
  - partial profits around risk-based targets
  - then manage the rest as a trend if breakout confirms

## Main Challenge

- Anticipation is early by definition.
- The trader must avoid getting shaken out by normal base noise while still recognizing real breakdown of the setup.

## Rule Extracted

- `A prebreakout base lets me improve location, but only if the level is truly important and the base shows real pressure into that level.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is useful for box-type structures before `Last Kiss` style resolution.
- It is more advanced than waiting for confirmed breakout and retest, but can improve entry quality when the base is exceptionally clean.
