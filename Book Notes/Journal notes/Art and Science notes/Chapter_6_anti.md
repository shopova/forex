Section focus:
- `The Anti`

## Core Idea

- The `Anti` is the first pullback after a possible trend change.
- It is a structured way to trade trend termination rather than blindly fading an overextended move.
- The sequence matters more than the signal candle.

## Setup

- First, there must be a reason to suspect trend termination:
  - exhaustion
  - overextension
  - double top/bottom
  - failure test
  - loss of momentum
- Second, the market must show a distinct change of character:
  - strong countertrend impulse
  - larger than prior countertrend moves
  - visible shift in momentum

## Trigger

- Enter on the first pullback after that strong countertrend impulse.
- It can be traded with the same tools used for pullbacks:
  - support/resistance
  - breakout trigger
- The difference is context:
  - it may be the first pullback in a new trend rather than continuation in the old one

## Stop

- Conservative stop:
  - beyond the old trend extreme
- More aggressive stop:
  - closer, using pullback logic
- Because this is countertrend / early reversal logic, stops must be respected without exception.

## Profit Target

- Countertrend trades demand lower expectations and tighter protection.
- One good plan:
  - take meaningful partial profits early
  - keep some exposure for the possibility that a real new trend develops
- Watch the measured move objective carefully:
  - failure there can signal complex consolidation instead of true reversal

## Rule Extracted

- `Do not trade reversal just because a market looks tired; trade the first pullback only after a real change of character has already appeared.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is the cleanest Chapter 6 template for selective reversal work in your framework.
- It is more relevant to aggressive reversal situations than to your default continuation model.
