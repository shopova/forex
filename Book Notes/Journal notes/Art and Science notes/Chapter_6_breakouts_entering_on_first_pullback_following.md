Parent chapter:
- `Chapter 6: Practical Trading Templates`

Section focus:
- `Breakouts, Entering on First Pullback Following`

## Core Idea

- This is the safer post-breakout entry.
- The actual breakout is already proven; the trader then enters on the first pullback in the new trend.
- Structurally, it is just a pullback trade in breakout context.

## Setup

- A successful breakout of an important level has already happened.
- Good activity, volatility, and acceptance beyond the level should be visible.
- The first reaction after the breakout begins.

## Trigger

- Treat the first pullback like a normal pullback.
- Use the same pullback triggers you would use in any trend trade.

## Stop

- Use normal pullback-style stops.
- The breakout level itself is usually a poor stop reference.
- A better structural reference is often the prebreakout pivot or base low/high.

## Profit Target

- Same logic as other pullback trades.
- One caveat:
  - good breakout trends can become exceptional trends, so it makes sense to keep some exposure for a larger move

## Main Challenge

- Avoid overcomplicating the trade.
- Once the breakout is confirmed, the first pullback is still just a pullback.

## Rule Extracted

- `The first pullback after a valid breakout should be traded like a pullback, not micromanaged like a breakout panic.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is one of the closest chapter templates to your breakout-following continuation work.
- In your framework, this often aligns naturally with confirmed breakout, retest, and continuation logic.
