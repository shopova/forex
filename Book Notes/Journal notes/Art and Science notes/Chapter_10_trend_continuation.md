Section focus:
- `Trend Continuation`

## Core Idea

- Trend continuation trades are the book's clearest examples of repeatable swing opportunities.
- The best ones align:
  - with bigger-picture structure
  - with a clean pullback or consolidation
  - with immediate follow-through after entry
- The trade does not need to capture the entire trend. It only needs to capture the next clean swing.

## Main Lessons

- `Context strengthens simple patterns`
  - A simple pullback becomes much stronger when higher-time-frame structure supports the direction.
  - Overextension or reversal pressure on a bigger time frame can also strengthen continuation in the opposite direction after character changes.

- `Continuation should move`
  - Once the trade triggers, price should behave with urgency.
  - If the market stalls for too long, the edge may already be gone.

- `Intraday structure can refine daily setups`
  - Even when the trade is planned from a higher time frame, smaller-time-frame climaxes, failures, or consolidations can sharpen the actual entry.

- `One clean swing is enough`
  - Grimes repeatedly emphasizes taking the clean swing, not trying to extract every possible point from the entire move.
  - Reentries may exist, but they are separate decisions.

## What This Changes For Me

- I should value clean follow-through more than perfect visual pattern symmetry.
- If the continuation idea is correct, the trade should not need endless patience.
- The job is not to predict the entire trend path; it is to capture the next high-quality leg.

## Rule Extracted

- `A continuation trade should move cleanly after entry; if it does not, I should stop treating it like a clean continuation.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This fits your continuation framework directly:
  - retests
  - pullback entries
  - EMA-bounce continuation
  - breaker-style continuation triggers
- For FX, the best continuation trades usually show:
  - clean rejection from the pullback area
  - room to the next opposing structure
  - fast acceptance in the trend direction
- If the pair churns after entry instead of expanding, it is often a warning that the setup quality was overstated.
