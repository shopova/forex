Section focus:
- `Pullback, Buying Support or Shorting Resistance`

## Core Idea

- This is the classic continuation trade.
- The trader uses a corrective pullback to position into the larger trend at a better price.
- The exact shape of the pullback matters less than its function as continuation structure.

## Setup

- The market must be trending.
- The setup leg before the pullback should show good momentum.
- No major contradictory conditions should dominate:
  - momentum divergence
  - obvious overextension
  - loss of trend integrity
- Good pullbacks usually show reduced activity and weak countertrend pressure.

## Trigger

- Two main entries:
  - buy/sell against the pullback edge itself
  - buy/sell after a small failure beyond the pullback support/resistance and quick recovery
- Avoid repeated tests of the same pullback edge; `3+` tests often weaken the pattern.

## Stop

- Grimes prefers stops that respect pattern geometry rather than ultra-tight stops.
- Very tight stops may reduce nominal loss size but raise stop-out probability.
- Add some distance beyond obvious stop clusters where possible.

## Profit Target

- Conservative target:
  - previous swing extreme
- Other target:
  - measured move objective
- Practical approach:
  - take some partial profit and leave room for another trend leg

## Main Challenge

- Distinguishing a healthy strong trend from a mature, overextended one.
- Timing entry inside the pullback is difficult because the pullback itself is noisy.
- Stop placement is tricky because lower-time-frame countertrend action can stretch further than expected.

## Rule Extracted

- `A pullback trade is valid only when the setup leg has good momentum and the pullback stays corrective.`

## Practical Forex Translation

- This is the template most directly connected to your continuation work.
- Your `Last Kiss` and other continuation entries should only be taken when the pullback still looks like continuation, not like failure or climax.
