Risk Scaling Plan

Core Principle  
Risk increases only after proven execution and statistical consistency, never after emotion or short-term profit.

Stage 1 — Proving Stage (€500 → \~€650)

* Risk per trade: 1% fixed  
* Only A- and B-grade trades  
* Max 2 concurrent trades

Remain here until: \- 50 live trades completed \- Expectancy \> \+0.30R \- ≤1 rule violation per 20 trades \-  
Max drawdown ≤10%

Stage 2 — Performance Stage (\~€650 → \~€1,000)

* Default risk: 1%  
* A+ setups: 1.5% (max one at a time)  
* Max total exposure: 2.5%

A+ criteria must all be met (Wave 3, fib \+ EMA confluence, Last Kiss, momentum aligned).

Stage 3 — Consistency Stage (\~€1,000 → \~€2,000)

* Normal risk: 1.25%  
* A+ setups: 2% max

Entry requirements: \- 100+ live trades \- 3 consecutive profitable months \- Expectancy \> \+0.40R \- Emotional stability during losses

Stage 4 — Capital Preservation Mode (Any Time)  
Triggered if: \- 3 consecutive losses \- 6% drawdown from equity high \- 2 rule violations in a week \-  
Emotional pressure present  
Action: \- Reduce risk to 0.5% \- Trade only Wave 3 \+ Last Kiss \- Minimum 10 trades before restoring risk

Rules That Never Change

* Risk never increases after wins or losses  
* Position size adjusts automatically with balance  
* Monthly risk audit required

Goal: survive long enough for compounding to matter
