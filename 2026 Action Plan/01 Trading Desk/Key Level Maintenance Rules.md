# Key Level Maintenance Rules

Use structure, not time, as the main rule.

## Daily Levels

Update only when one of these happens:

- price makes a major new swing high or low
- a daily level is decisively broken and later no longer acts as support / resistance
- a newer daily turning point becomes more important than the old one
- the market transitions into a clearly different range or trend structure

So daily levels can stay for weeks or months, but they should be reviewed every weekend.

## 4H Levels

Review more often, usually every few days or at least before each trading week.

Update when:

- a `4H` swing has clearly formed
- the old level has been broken and is no longer respected
- a prior level has flipped from support to resistance or vice versa
- a newer `4H` reaction is now more tradable than the older one

These should not be redrawn constantly, but they should not be left untouched just because they are old.

## 30m Levels

These are tactical.

Update frequently:

- at the start of the session
- after a clear intraday impulse
- after a reclaim / failed break / consolidation edge forms
- when the execution structure changes

These are the levels most allowed to be temporary.

## Best Routine

1. Weekend:
   Review and clean `Daily` and major `4H` levels.
2. Each trading day:
   Check whether `4H` structure has changed enough to add / remove a level.
3. Each session:
   Mark fresh `30m` execution levels only if they matter relative to the HTF map.

## Important Rule

Do not keep too many old levels just because they once mattered.

A level becomes weaker when:

- price trades through it repeatedly
- it sits in the middle of messy range noise
- newer structure has overtaken it

## Summary

- `Daily`: rarely change, but review weekly
- `4H`: change selectively, review every few days / each week
- `30m`: update often, based on current execution structure
