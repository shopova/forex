# Pre-Trade Entry Filter

Use this note right before any trade.

Purpose:

- stop low-quality entries before they happen
- keep the `30m` in its proper role as a trigger chart
- force trades to come from your actual edge, not from impatience

This is the standard:

- `Daily` gives the map
- `4H` gives the condition and zone
- `30m` gives the trigger

If the `30m` is trying to invent the trade on its own, there is no trade.

## Your Actual Edge

Your best trades come from:

- pre-marked `Daily` or `4H` level
- supportive or clearly understandable higher-time-frame context
- reclaim, retest, acceptance, or breakout retest behavior
- stop-order or confirmed entry after price proves the idea
- clean projected reward-to-risk of at least `2R`

Your weak trades usually come from:

- second touch entries
- fading momentum without real control shift
- `30m` reactions inside mixed higher-time-frame structure
- mid-range entries
- late breakout chasing

## The Five-Box Filter

Before entry, all `5` boxes must be `Yes`.

### 1. Is the level meaningful?

Allowed sources:

- `Daily` or `4H` swing high / low
- repeated support or resistance
- reclaim area
- breakout / failed-breakout area
- clear supply / demand zone

Not enough:

- random `30m` line
- middle of chop
- level added after you already wanted the trade

If `No`:

- no trade

### 2. Is the higher-time-frame context supportive?

Allowed:

- `Daily` and `4H` aligned
- `Daily` neutral but `4H` clearly improving from support
- `Daily` neutral but price is at a real range edge with strong reversal potential

Weak:

- `Daily` mixed and `4H` mixed
- price is in the middle of a broad range
- your direction fights clear higher-time-frame pressure

Rule:

- if the trade is countertrend, the trigger must be unusually strong

If `No`:

- no trade

### 3. Is price at a location that deserves a trade?

Good location:

- range edge
- reclaim zone
- breakout retest zone
- support after healthy pullback
- resistance after healthy pullback

Bad location:

- middle of the range
- after a long move already happened
- level is being traded through repeatedly
- the market is overlapping and messy

If `No`:

- no trade

### 4. Is the `30m` trigger complete?

Only these triggers are allowed:

- `Level reclaim`
- `Micro structure break`

Valid `Level reclaim`:

- price trades through the level
- then closes back through it
- then shows re-acceptance on the reclaimed side

Valid `Micro structure break`:

- price reacts at the meaningful level first
- then breaks the last meaningful local pivot
- the break closes through the pivot

Not enough:

- second touch
- hesitation
- one wick
- momentum looks tired
- one candle looks nice
- penetration without acceptance

If `No`:

- no trade

### 5. Is the trade still clean after confirmation?

You need:

- structural invalidation
- stop placed where the thesis is actually wrong
- target at a logical level
- projected reward-to-risk `>= 2R`

Warning:

- if waiting for confirmation destroys the `R:R`, skip
- if the nearest logical target is too close, skip

If `No`:

- no trade

## Traffic-Light Rule

### Green Light

All of these are true:

- meaningful level
- supportive higher-time-frame context
- good location
- trigger complete
- structural invalidation
- clean `>= 2R`

Action:

- allowed to place the order

### Yellow Light

One part is weak, but not fully broken.

Examples:

- location is decent but not great
- trigger exists but the structure is still a little noisy
- range environment reduces clarity

Action:

- do not place the order yet
- wait for more proof or skip

### Red Light

Any of these is true:

- level is weak
- trade is mid-range
- trigger is incomplete
- trade depends on anticipation
- no clean `2R`
- trade is mostly based on fading momentum

Action:

- no trade

## Hard Skip Rules

Skip immediately if:

- the `30m` is trying to overpower unclear `Daily` / `4H`
- you are entering because of a `second touch`
- you are entering because price `looks tired`
- price is still inside obvious overlap
- the level was not marked before the session
- price already made the move and you are chasing late
- the setup needs an exact line rather than a real zone
- major news is too close
- you cannot explain the trade in one sentence

## One-Sentence Thesis Rule

Before entry, complete this sentence:

`I am buying / selling because price is at [meaningful HTF level], the HTF context is [supportive reason], the 30m has confirmed through [reclaim / structure break], invalidation is [level], and there is room to [target] for at least 2R.`

If you cannot say it clearly:

- no trade

## Entry Sequence

Use this exact order.

1. Mark the `Daily` and `4H` level before the session.
2. Decide whether the market is:
   - continuation
   - reclaim
   - breakout retest
   - sweep reversal
   - no trade
3. Decide whether location is:
   - range edge
   - meaningful pullback zone
   - messy middle
4. Wait for the `30m` trigger candle to close.
5. Define invalidation before placing the order.
6. Check whether target still gives `>= 2R`.
7. Place the order only if all boxes are `Yes`.

## Best Possible Conditions

These are the conditions you should trust most.

### Best Longs

- `Daily` / `4H` support or reclaim zone
- bullish or recovering higher-time-frame structure
- controlled pullback
- `30m` reclaim or break of last lower high
- room to next resistance for `>= 2R`

### Best Shorts

- `Daily` / `4H` resistance or failed-break zone
- bearish or weakening higher-time-frame structure
- controlled rally into resistance
- `30m` reclaim or break of last higher low
- room to next support for `>= 2R`

### Best Range Reversals

- price is at a real `Daily` or `4H` range edge
- move into the edge is stretched
- edge gets swept and rejected
- price re-accepts back inside the range
- `30m` confirms away from the edge

## What Your 2024 Trades Taught

The strong trade had:

- reclaim at a meaningful `4H` level
- aligned context
- stop-order confirmation

The weak trades had one or more of these:

- `tag` instead of reclaim
- `second touch` instead of confirmation
- momentum fade instead of bearish control
- countertrend idea without strong evidence
- late continuation without retest
- `30m` trigger trying to compensate for weak location

## Final Gate

If this trade loses, what do you want the reason to be?

Acceptable reason:

- the market proved you wrong after a real setup

Unacceptable reason:

- you entered before the setup was actually proven

If the likely reason for loss is impatience:

- no trade
