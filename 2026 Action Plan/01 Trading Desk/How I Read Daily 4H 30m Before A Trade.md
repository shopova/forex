# How I Read Daily 4H 30m Before A Trade

## Purpose
This note is not about finding a pattern quickly.

It is about reading the market in the correct order so the `30m` does not invent trades that the higher time frames do not support.

The basic rule is:

- `Daily` gives the environment
- `4H` gives the decision area and likely scenario
- `30m` gives the trigger and the actual trade

If `Daily` and `4H` do not create a real candidate, I should not hunt on the `30m`.

## Step 1: Read The Daily Chart
On the `Daily`, I am not looking for an entry.

I am asking:

- Is the market `bullish`, `bearish`, or `mixed`?
- Is it `trending`, `ranging`, or `transitioning`?
- Where are the important `Daily` levels?
- Is price near a meaningful area, or just somewhere in the middle?

Example:

- Price has been falling for a while
- Bearish structure is still intact
- There is no real bullish control yet

My conclusion should be:

- `Prefer shorts`
- `Be careful with longs`
- `Do not let a local 30m bounce talk me into a reversal`

Important:

- A slowing bearish move is not automatically bullish
- A slowing bullish move is not automatically bearish

## Step 2: Read The 4H Chart
On the `4H`, I decide whether price is actually at a tradeable area.

I am asking:

- Is price at a meaningful `4H` level or zone?
- Is this more likely a `continuation area`, a `reaction area`, or a `possible reversal area`?
- Is price accepting above or below the level, or only touching it?
- If a reversal happens here, would there still be clean room for `>= 2R`?

Example:

- Daily is bearish
- Price is now sitting at a `4H` level
- The bearish move may be slowing

Correct conclusion:

- I do not automatically buy
- I do not automatically sell either
- I wait for price to prove which side has control

This is a key rule:

`If I cannot clearly say which side has control, I am not at the 30m trigger stage yet.`

## Step 3: Decide Whether The 30m Even Matters
I do not go to the `30m` because the market is open.

I go to the `30m` only if `Daily + 4H` created a real candidate.

That means:

- If the higher time frames are not at a meaningful area, I do not hunt on the `30m`
- If the higher time frames are mixed and messy, I trade less or not at all
- If the higher time frames are clear, I go to the `30m` and wait for proof

So each day the process is:

1. Check `Daily`
2. Check `4H`
3. Decide whether there is a real setup candidate
4. If not, no reason to focus on the `30m` for entries

## Step 4: What The 30m Is For
The `30m` is not for finding random patterns.

The `30m` is for confirming whether the higher-time-frame idea is actually happening.

On the `30m`, I want one of these:

- `acceptance`
- `reclaim`
- `retest hold`
- `failed reclaim`
- `break and hold`

What the `30m` must answer:

- Has price actually proven the trade?
- Is the market now treating the level as support or resistance?
- Is this clean enough to enter?

What the `30m` must not do:

- invent a reversal against strong HTF structure
- turn hesitation into a trade
- make one strong candle look like full confirmation

## Example Workflow
### Case: Daily And 4H Are Bearish
If `Daily` and `4H` are bearish:

- my default job is to look for `30m` shorts
- I do not look for longs unless reversal evidence is unusually strong

So if price comes into a `4H` level from below:

- if price rejects it and stays below, I can look for a `30m` short
- if price reclaims above it and holds, I may later consider a long
- if price chops around it, I do nothing

### Case: Price Reclaims The 4H Level But 30m Is Flat
This is important.

Sometimes the `4H` gives a possible bullish idea:

- price gets above the `4H` level
- the level was meaningful
- a retouch idea exists

But then on the `30m`:

- price is flat
- candles overlap
- there is no clean continuation

Correct conclusion:

- the idea is not dead yet
- but the trigger is not clean yet
- I wait

This is much better than forcing a long because I already liked the `4H` idea.

## Strong Candle Rule
This is one of the most important corrections.

Do not say:

- `I saw a strong candle, so I entered`

Say:

- price reclaimed the level
- price held above it
- `30m` started treating it as support
- then a strong candle broke local structure in the trade direction
- then I entered

So the strong candle is not the reason by itself.
It is only useful if it comes after proper context and confirmation.

## How I Decide On Direction
I should ask:

- What side am I allowed to prefer here?
- Has price actually proved that side on the `30m`?

That means:

- `Daily` and `4H` give me permission
- `30m` gives me execution

Example:

- `Daily bearish + 4H bearish` -> prefer `30m` shorts
- `Daily bullish + 4H bullish` -> prefer `30m` longs
- `Daily mixed + 4H mixed` -> trade less, require stronger location and confirmation

## Stop Placement
My stop should not go somewhere random.

The stop should be under or above the structure that would actually invalidate the trade.

Before placing the stop, ask:

- If this level breaks, is my trade idea actually wrong?
- Is this stop beyond the reclaim / hold structure?
- Does the setup still leave clean `>= 2R`?

If the stop has to go so far that `2R` disappears, I skip the trade.

## Full Practical Sequence
Use this before every trade:

1. On `Daily`, define:
   - bullish, bearish, or mixed
   - trend, range, or transition
   - important levels

2. On `4H`, define:
   - where price is now
   - whether this is continuation, reaction, or possible reversal
   - whether there is enough room for `>= 2R`

3. If `Daily + 4H` do not create a real candidate, do not hunt on `30m`

4. If they do create a candidate, go to `30m` and wait for one of:
   - acceptance
   - reclaim
   - retest hold
   - failed reclaim
   - break and hold

5. Enter only if:
   - the level is meaningful
   - the context supports the trade
   - the `30m` proves the idea
   - the stop is structural
   - `>= 2R` still exists

## Fast Reminder
- `Daily` tells me the environment
- `4H` tells me the zone and likely scenario
- `30m` tells me whether the market has earned my entry

- hesitation is not reversal
- a strong candle is not enough
- a flat `30m` after a reclaim means wait, not force
- no trade is a valid decision
