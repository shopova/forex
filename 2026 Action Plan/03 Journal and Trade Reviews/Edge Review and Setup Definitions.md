## Edge Rules

Only risk money when all of these are present:

1. A pre-marked `Daily` or `4H` level that still matters.
2. A higher-time-frame context that is aligned, or at least clearly supportive.
3. A `30m` trigger that is complete, not anticipated.
4. Structural invalidation.
5. Projected reward-to-risk of at least `2R`.

Additional rule:

- if the setup is countertrend, the reversal evidence must be unusually strong

Hard skip rules:

- no trade if the `30m` is trying to overpower unclear `Daily` / `4H`
- no trade just because momentum looks tired
- no trade if you cannot explain in one sentence why the level matters
- no trade if the setup depends on price touching an exact line rather than reacting to a real zone

## Definitions: What You Should Be Looking For

These are the elements that should appear on the chart before a setup becomes tradable.

### Meaningful Level

Definition:

- a level that matters on `Daily` or `4H`
- not just a line from recent `30m` noise

Common signs:

- prior swing high or low
- repeated support or resistance reaction
- breakout or failed breakout area
- reclaim area that changed behavior previously

Not enough:

- a line drawn through the middle of messy overlap
- a level that price keeps trading through without reaction

### Higher-Time-Frame Alignment

Definition:

- `Daily` and `4H` do not need to be perfectly identical, but they must support the trade or at least not fight it strongly

Aligned:

- bullish continuation long in bullish or recovering structure
- bearish continuation short in bearish or weakening structure

Mixed:

- `Daily` neutral but `4H` improving
- `Daily` bullish but `4H` correcting

Countertrend:

- long into clear higher-time-frame bearish pressure
- short into clear higher-time-frame bullish pressure

Rule:

- the more countertrend the setup is, the stronger the trigger must be

### Acceptance

Definition:

- price is not only touching or wicking a level
- it is showing the ability to stay on the intended side of that level

What it looks like:

- close through the level
- follow-through after the close
- higher lows above reclaimed support or lower highs below reclaimed resistance

Not enough:

- one wick
- one dramatic candle with no follow-through
- chop around the level

### Reclaim

Definition:

- price trades through a meaningful level
- then closes back through it
- then shows re-acceptance on the reclaimed side

Bullish reclaim:

- support is briefly lost
- price closes back above
- price then holds above support

Bearish reclaim:

- resistance is briefly broken
- price closes back below
- price then holds below resistance

### Retest

Definition:

- after a break or reclaim, price returns toward the level in a controlled way
- the market tests whether the new side of the level will hold

Healthy retest:

- controlled pullback
- no violent rejection against the idea
- continuation can restart from there

Bad retest:

- pullback is too deep
- price loses the reclaimed area
- correction becomes impulsive against the trade

### Micro Structure Break

Definition:

- price reacts at a meaningful level
- then breaks the last meaningful local pivot in the intended direction

Bullish:

- support holds
- price breaks the last `30m` lower high

Bearish:

- resistance holds
- price breaks the last `30m` higher low

Rule:

- if the pivot is too minor to matter, the break is not enough

### Continuation Behavior

Definition:

- after entry, price should behave like the original thesis

Healthy continuation:

- timely expansion
- reduced overlap
- progress away from entry
- structure building in the trade direction

Deteriorating continuation:

- long stall near entry
- repeated overlap
- weak breakout attempts
- failure to extend after a reasonable amount of time

Rule:

- if the thesis is still healthy, manage with stop
- if the thesis is no longer healthy, consider exiting instead of only moving the stop

### Bullish Control

Definition:

- buyers are no longer just slowing the selloff
- they are actively taking control

Signs:

- reclaim of support
- break of local lower highs
- higher lows forming
- closes holding above important levels

Not the same as:

- bearish momentum fading
- one bounce in a range

### Bearish Control

Definition:

- sellers are no longer just slowing the rally
- they are actively taking control

Signs:

- reclaim below resistance
- break of local higher lows
- lower highs forming
- closes holding below important levels

Not the same as:

- bullish momentum fading
- one hesitation candle under resistance

### Rotational / Noisy Market

Definition:

- price is moving back and forth without clean directional control

Common signs:

- overlapping candles
- repeated trading through nearby levels
- flat moving averages
- price stuck in the middle of a range

Rule:

- if the market is rotational, the `30m` trigger must be exceptional or the trade should be skipped

### Structural Invalidation

Definition:

- the point where the trade idea is logically wrong, not merely uncomfortable

Examples:

- long loses reclaimed support
- short loses reclaimed resistance
- continuation trade fails and breaks the key retest structure

Rule:

- stop goes where the setup is wrong
- not where the lot size feels better

### Setup Quality / Room to Target

Definition:

- a setup is only live-model quality if price has enough room to produce at least `2R`

Rule:

- target into the opposing zone, not fantasy extension through it
- if confirmation destroys `R:R`, skip

### Candidate vs Valid Setup

Candidate:

- interesting context
- meaningful level
- maybe a recognizable pattern
- but no complete trigger yet

Valid setup:

- context is clear
- level is meaningful
- trigger is complete
- invalidation is structural
- target still offers `>= 2R`

## Practical Edge Checklist

Before entry, ask:

1. What is the level, and why does it matter on `Daily` or `4H`?
2. Is the higher-time-frame context aligned, supportive, mixed, or countertrend?
3. Is this a reclaim, retest, or micro structure break, or am I just reacting to candle shape?
4. Has control actually changed?
5. Where is the structural invalidation?
6. Is there still real room to `2R`?
7. If this trade stalls, what behavior would tell me the thesis has degraded?

If you cannot answer those clearly, the setup is not ready.
